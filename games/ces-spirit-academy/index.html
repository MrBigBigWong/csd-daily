How to Play

  • Choose a Gold / Wood / Water / Fire / Earth Spirit and raise it at the academy home.
  • Use daily Care (snack / cheer / train) and Train Move for practice FX.
  • Open the Quest Map. Stages unlock in order across Chapter 1 (Module 2.1) and Chapter 2 (Module 2.2).
  • In battle: pick a skill, then answer a CES question to cast. Correct = big hit + combo; wrong = miss and the foe may strike.
  • Win by reducing foe HP to 0. First clear of a stage grants XP; bosses give badges.
  • Daily Quest (once per HK day) boosts streak. Practice is a lighter non-HP quiz.
  • Get 10 correct in a qualifying run to open a blind-box item (1.5× XP, Sure Cast, or Extra Care).
  • Max level 100. Each Class + Student No. has its own save on this device.
